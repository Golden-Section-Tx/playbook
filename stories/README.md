# Stories

A mistake in [MISTAKES.md](../MISTAKES.md) is two sentences. A story is what
those two sentences cost somebody.

This folder holds longer write-ups attached to individual mistakes: what the
situation looked like from inside, what the early signals were, what it cost,
and what caught it — or what it took to recover when nothing did. The list tells
you the mistake exists. A story is what makes you recognise it in your own
company.

Empty for now. It fills as stories can be told properly.

## Naming

One file per story, named for the mistake it belongs to:

```
stories/m016-the-quarter-we-stopped-counting.md
```

Several stories may attach to the same mistake. Link each one from that
mistake's entry in `MISTAKES.md`.

## The rule that matters

**Anonymised, or not published.** No company names. No founder names. No
figures precise enough to identify a company, and no combination of details
that does the same work a name would — a sector, a round size, a city, and a
year together identify a company as surely as its name does.

If a founder's own story is being told and they want their name on it, that
takes their written permission, on the record, for that specific text. In every
other case the story is told with the identifying detail removed, or it is not
told here.

Nothing in this folder may be a verbatim quote from a private conversation.
Essence, in the writer's own words. That constraint applies to Golden Section
more strictly than to anyone else contributing, because Golden Section is the
one with access to the conversations.

## Shape

No fixed template. What tends to work:

- The situation, as it looked at the time — not as it looks in hindsight.
- The signals that were visible and were rationalised away.
- What it cost. Money, months, people, or the company.
- What actually changed things, and how long it took to work.
- The play that would have prevented it, and honestly whether running it would
  have been enough.

Written in the second person, present tense, without a moral at the end.
Founders can draw their own conclusions; they are good at it. See
[CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md) for the mechanics.
