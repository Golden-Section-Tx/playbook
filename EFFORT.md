# How effort is estimated

Every play carries two estimates. `initialEffort` is what it costs to stand
the play up the first time. `ongoingEffort` is what one recurrence costs at
the play's cadence. Both are in story points.

Generated by `scripts/build.mjs` — do not edit by hand.

## The scale

**A point is one person's working time.** Not elapsed calendar time, and not
the team's combined time: two people for a day is two days of effort, not one.

| SP | Effort | Person-days |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | One meeting | 0.25 |
| 2 | Half a day | 0.5 |
| 3 | One day | 1 |
| 5 | One to two days | 1.5 |
| 8 | Half a week | 2.5 |
| 13 | One week | 5 |
| 21 | Two weeks | 10 |
| 34 | Three weeks | 15 |

## Initial and ongoing are different questions

`ongoingEffort` is the cost of *one* recurrence, so the annual load is that
cost times the cadence. 8 SP monthly is two and a half person-days, twelve
times a year — about six person-weeks. A play whose stand-up is affordable
and whose recurrence is not is the most common way a company ends up
overcommitted, which is why the two numbers are quoted separately everywhere.

## Points size one play; days add plays together

The point-to-day curve is deliberately not linear. 13 to 21 SP is 62% more
points but twice the days; 21 to 34 SP is 62% more points and half again the
days. Story points are a sizing vocabulary for a single play and they do not
sum. **To weigh a set of plays against what a team has, convert each to
person-days and add those.**

## Points are not a schedule

Effort says how much work there is, not when it will be finished. Elapsed
time depends on how much of the week the owner actually has for it. Ten
person-days for a CFO with two days a week is five calendar weeks, not two.
Any plan built from a play has to say which availability it assumed.

## Why the scale stops at 34

Three person-weeks is the largest honest estimate for a single play. Past
that it is more than one play and should be split, and a larger number would
only make the split easier to avoid.

`initialEffort` and `ongoingEffort` take one of the eight values above, or an
em dash where the effort is not meaningfully bounded — a continuous play like
Executive Execution has no stand-up cost to quote.

## The estimates are claims, and they can be wrong

The numbers come from Golden Section's experience of these plays across its
portfolio, not from a model. If you run a play and it costs something else,
that is the most useful thing you can send back: see
[CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) and the `field-report` skill.
